A music remote for whatever is playing on your phone, in a soft ocean look. The song title and artist sit under a
turning colour disc, with frosted round buttons for previous, play/pause and next. Behind them, teal, blue and green
glows drift slowly while the music plays, and the ring around play/pause spins. Pause, and everything holds still.

Turn the crown to change the volume: up is louder, down is quieter, with a small buzz each step.

When nothing is playing, Tide says so and waits. Press the crown or swipe right to leave. Tide can also be a page
next to your watch face: long press its row in Apps and choose "Add page".

## Requirements

- The [patched firmware](/firmware) and an [App Loader](/firmware) from 16 November 2026 or later (phone music).
  On an older loader Tide shows "Update loader".
- The watch connected to your phone in Nothing X, with music playing in any app.
- Crown volume needs a newer App Loader than 16 November 2026. Until then the crown shows "Update loader" and
  everything else works.
